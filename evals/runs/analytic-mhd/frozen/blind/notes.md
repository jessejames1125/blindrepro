- iota_uniform_family: computed by unwrapping the geometric poloidal angle theta = arg[(R-R_a) - i(z-Z_a(phi))] and phi along field lines from eq. embedding (eps=1/2) on 16 (psi, alpha) samples; Z_a(phi) = (eps/2) sin 2phi because phi = zeta on the axis (x,y ∝ cos,sin zeta). Field lines close after one zeta period, so the turn counts are integers; the reported value is the ratio of the rounded integer turn counts (raw ratios differ from it by about 3e-15).
- Force residuals: derivatives come from a hand-written forward-mode automatic-differentiation class over complex numbers (exact first derivatives, double precision). Only first derivatives are needed, since curl B and grad p each involve one derivative. The values are at round-off level (~1e-15), so their exact digits depend on the implementation and the order of floating-point operations.
- Sheared family: the sqrt in K uses numpy's principal branch, which has Re >= 0 as the paper specifies. I set p_a = 0 (it is irrelevant). Per the spec, lambda = 1.
- Sheared family sample points: these come from eq. sheared_embedding with eq. sheared_sigma. A check confirms psi = k_b^2/2 at each point.
- iota_sheared_axis = h0*H(eps), with H found by adaptive quadrature (scipy quad). I cross-checked it against the full ODE at k=1e-5, and the two agree to 1e-11.
- iota_sheared_edge: I computed the Poincaré map over one zeta-period pi of the chi ODE with DOP853 (rtol = atol = 1e-13) on a uniform chi grid of 128 points. The ODE depends on zeta only through sin2zeta and cos2zeta, so the period is pi. I represented F(chi)-chi with a Fourier series (the tail coefficients are about 1e-14) and iterated it 1e5 times. The rotation number comes from a weighted Birkhoff average using the exp(-1/(t(1-t))) weight; this method is from Das, Sander and Yorke, recalled from memory and not from the paper. Then iota = (mean increment per pi)/pi.
- Cross-checks for iota_sheared_edge: a 64-point grid with 5e4 iterations agrees to about 2e-15, and direct ODE integration over 400 half-transits agrees to about 6e-12.
